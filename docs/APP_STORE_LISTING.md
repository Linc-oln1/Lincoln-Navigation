## Description (4000)

Lincoln Navigation is a maps and navigation app built for Ghana. Plan a route, follow turn-by-turn directions and see what is ahead, whether you are driving through Accra, taking a trotro to Kumasi or walking across town.

GETTING AROUND
• Directions for five ways to travel: car, trotro, motorbike, bicycle and walking
• Turn-by-turn guidance that follows your position
• Search for places across Ghana, including GhanaPost GPS digital addresses
• Explore nearby restaurants, banks, fuel stations, hospitals, hotels and more
• Drop a pin anywhere on the map and get directions to it

KNOW WHAT IS AHEAD
• Road hazard reports from other travellers: flooding, police checkpoints, bad road surface, crashes and road closures
• Warnings when your route passes a reported hazard
• Weather for the area you are looking at
• Share your live trip so family and friends can follow it

BUILT FOR GHANA
• Search and place data focused on Ghana
• Route guides on our website for the roads people actually travel, like Accra to Kumasi and Kumasi to Tamale
• Available in English and several other languages

FREE TO USE
The map, search, directions and hazard reports are free. Paid plans for extra features are sold on our website, not inside this app.

Reports from other travellers are not verified and are shown as they were submitted. Never use your phone while driving. Please follow the road signs and the law.

Lincoln Navigation is built by Jonathan Lincoln Enterprise, a Ghana-founded technology company.
