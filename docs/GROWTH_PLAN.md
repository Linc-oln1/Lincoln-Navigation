# 30-day growth plan (7 Oct to 6 Nov 2026)

A plan to react to, not a promise. Anything marked **(guess)** is my judgement, not something I measured.
Companion files: `docs/OWNER_CHECKLIST.md` (what to do and when) and `docs/SOCIAL_POSTS.md` (16 days of posts).

## Where you are

- About **90 real Ghana visitors a month**. Google Search Console (30 Sep to 4 Oct): 85 impressions, 16 clicks, all from searches for the site itself. The route guides were shown but not clicked yet because they are new.
- 69% of visitors are on phones and 61% on iPhones.
- Assets you already have: 23 route guides and Boti Falls (all submitted to Google), a share-ready preview image on every page, 16 social posts, a referral reward (7 free Pro days for both people), a promoter programme (`/ambassador`), one paying sponsor, an Android app in closed testing.
- Nothing is wrong with the product. The problem is that few people know it exists. So the plan is mostly about being seen where Ghanaians already look for directions.

## What "working" looks like in 30 days (guess)

These are small on purpose. Setting a number you can reach tells you more than a big one you can't.

| Measure | Now | Target by 6 Nov | Where to read it |

## The four weeks

**Week 1 (7 to 13 Oct): start the habit and fix the loose ends**
- Publish Day 1 to Day 7 from the social posts, one per day, in two or three groups.
- Message Franchman for their details (the message is in the checklist).
- Start recruiting Android testers from the people who respond to your posts.
- Do one referral signup by hand to confirm it works before you mention the free Pro days anywhere.

**Week 2 (14 to 20 Oct): read the results, double down**
- Around 15 Oct, check which posts and which group brought visitors. Do more of that kind of post and drop the rest.
- Publish Day 8 to Day 14.
- Pick two or three route guides that got Google impressions and share those again, with a new question.
- Ask five people you know to share one post in their own WhatsApp groups.

**Week 3 (21 to 27 Oct): add a second channel**
- Add the channel that looked best in week 2 (see the list below) and keep the daily posts going.
- Contact two or three local businesses about a sponsored listing, using Franchman as the example once they confirm their details and you have their number.
- If the Play test has 12 testers, note the date the 14 days end.

**Week 4 (28 Oct to 6 Nov): decide what to keep**
- Compare the table above with the real numbers.
- For each channel, keep, change or drop it (rules below).
- Write down three things people said or asked in comments. Those are your next features and posts.

## Channels, ranked by effort against payoff (guess)

1. **Facebook and WhatsApp groups about Ghana travel, trotro and commuting** (low effort, highest likelihood). It's where your posts are already written. Join as yourself, post one useful thing a day, answer comments. Don't post the same text in many groups on the same day.
2. **University groups** (KNUST, University of Ghana, UCC). Students travel between cities constantly and share links. Start with one class group or hostel chat you can join. Ask a classmate or friend to post the Accra to Kumasi guide.
3. **Local creators and travel pages.** Offer a feature: send a short message to a Ghana travel or road-trip page with one guide they could share. Keep it a single, specific ask. (Response rate is a guess; expect most to say nothing.)
4. **Tour operators, car hire and bus companies.** They rank for the same searches. A link to your guide on their page is worth more than a post. Offer a sponsored listing or a simple mention swap.
5. **X (Twitter) and TikTok.** Use after the first three work. X posts are already written. TikTok needs short videos of the map in use, which is more effort; leave it until you see that people respond to the app, not only the guides.

## Partnerships worth trying

- **Sponsored listings** (GHS 200 local, GHS 600 city-wide per 30 days). Franchman's listing now shows on the main map screen, which makes the pitch stronger. Only pitch once their details are filled in and you can show a view count.
- **Promoter programme.** The code is built: a promoter gets GHS 1,000 for every 50 verified paying subscribers. Decide your limit before inviting anyone, because you pay out cash. I'd recruit one or two promoters you trust, with real followings, only after you've seen that sign-ups happen. Today I'd not recruit, because the product has no paying subscribers yet.
- **Referral reward** (7 free Pro days for both). Mention it in posts only after your own test signup works.

## Rules for dropping or keeping a channel

- A channel gets **two weeks** and at least **10 posts or messages**. If it produced no visitors you can see in analytics, drop it.
- If a channel gave **even a few** visitors, keep it and change the post, not the group.
- Don't add a new channel until the previous one has had its two weeks.
- If, by 6 Nov, visitors haven't moved at all, the problem is probably the message, not the effort. Try one different angle for two weeks, such as "Is this road passable right now?" posts using the hazard reports, before adding more channels.

## What would change this plan

- **AdSense approved:** more visitors start to earn a little. It doesn't change what you do.
- **Android testing completes:** a Play Store listing becomes a channel of its own. Put the store link in every post.
- **A post goes well:** copy its structure (a concrete fact plus a question) for the next ten.

## Honest risks

- Group admins may delete promotional posts. Post as a person, share useful facts, and read each group's rules first.
- Visitors from Ghana are only about 90 a month, so small random changes will look large. Look at the trend over a week, not a single day.
- Several things here rely on your time and your network, not on me. I can write, build and check, but I can't post for you.
