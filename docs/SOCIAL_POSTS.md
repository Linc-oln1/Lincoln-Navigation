## Day 12: Boti Falls
/places/boti

**Facebook:**

Looking for a day trip from Accra? Boti Falls is a pair of waterfalls in the forest of the Eastern Region, known locally as the "male" and "female" falls. You drive to Koforidua (about two hours from Accra) and continue towards Huhunya; the falls are signposted from the road.

Good to know: they are fullest during and just after the rainy season, and there are many steps down to the pool and back up, so wear comfortable shoes. Umbrella Rock and a three-headed palm tree are nearby too.
