## Day 1: Accra to Kumasi
/routes/accra-to-kumasi

Heading Accra → Kumasi? It's about 247 km on the N6, usually 4½–6 hours. Most of the lost time isn't the highway, it's getting out past Ofankor/Nsawam and into Kumasi from Ejisu.

I put together a guide with the towns you pass (Nsawam, Suhum, Nkawkaw, Konongo), the coach vs trotro options, and tips: https://www.lincolnnavigation.com/routes/accra-to-kumasi?utm_source=facebook

Free, no sign-up. If I got something wrong about the road, tell me and I'll fix it.

## Day 8: Kumasi to Cape Coast
/routes/kumasi-to-cape-coast

**Facebook:**

Kumasi → Cape Coast is about 213 km on the N8, usually 3½ to 4½ hours. The road goes south through Bekwai, Fomena, Assin Praso and Assin Fosu, then meets the coast road near Yamoransa.

Most of it is forest and farmland, with long single-lane stretches, so slow trucks are the main delay. Assin Fosu is the usual stop for fuel and food.

## Day 12: Boti Falls
/places/boti

**Facebook:**

Looking for a day trip from Accra? Boti Falls is a pair of waterfalls in the forest of the Eastern Region, known locally as the "male" and "female" falls. You drive to Koforidua (about two hours from Accra) and continue towards Huhunya; the falls are signposted from the road.

Good to know: they are fullest during and just after the rainy season, and there are many steps down to the pool and back up, so wear comfortable shoes. Umbrella Rock and a three-headed palm tree are nearby too.

Guide: https://www.lincolnnavigation.com/places/boti?utm_source=facebook
